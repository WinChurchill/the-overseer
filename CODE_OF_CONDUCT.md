# Code of conduct

OVERSEER is a small project built with testers and contributors. Everyone taking
part in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces is expected to
keep it a respectful, harassment-free place to work.

## Expected

- Be considerate and assume good intent. Critique code and behavior, not people.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and accept it the same way.
- Respect privacy: share sanitized evidence only, and never post someone else's
  sessions, paths, credentials or personal details.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and discriminatory language
  or imagery.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Threats, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/WinChurchill/the-overseer/security/advisories/new)
and say that it is a conduct report. The maintainer will review it, keep the
reporter's identity confidential, and may edit or remove content, warn, or block
participants from the project.

This applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
It is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
